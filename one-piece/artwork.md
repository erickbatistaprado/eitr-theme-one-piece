# One Piece artwork

The 9 wallpapers are art inspired by One Piece, shared here as fan content for
personal desktops, free of charge. Each picture belongs to its artist, and the work it
shows belongs to Eiichiro Oda, Shueisha and Toei Animation. This theme is not affiliated with or endorsed by any of them.

- `01-luffy-at-sea.jpg`: Luffy at sea; artist unknown
- `02-straw-hat-jolly-roger.jpg`: The Straw Hat Jolly Roger; artist unknown
- `03-luffy.jpg`: Luffy under the red moon; artist unknown
- `04-zoro.jpg`: Zoro under the red moon; artist unknown
- `05-nami.jpg`: Nami under the red moon; artist unknown
- `06-sanji.jpg`: Sanji under the red moon; artist unknown
- `07-chopper.jpg`: Chopper under the red moon; artist unknown
- `08-franky.jpg`: Franky under the red moon; artist unknown
- `09-ace.jpg`: Portgas D. Ace; artist unknown

If one of these is yours, open an issue to be credited by name or to have it removed.

The palette is drawn from these pictures: dark crimson background, moonlight text, blood moon red accent, scarlet, sea green, straw hat yellow, ocean blue, sunset violet and sea teal.
